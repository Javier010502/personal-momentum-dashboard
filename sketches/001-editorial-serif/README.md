## Variant 001 — Editorial Serif

### Design stance
A quiet document-like surface where the serif numeral does the talking. The gauge reads like a magazine folio number, not an instrument.

### Key choices
- Accent: oxblood `#9E2B25` (restrained, used only on the gauge arc, chart line, category bars, and check-in fills)
- Type: Newsreader (editorial serif) for headings/numbers; Archivo (grotesk) for body/UI; JetBrains Mono for meta labels
- Gauge: slim 270-degree arc (135deg sweep), thin 10px stroke, large serif numeral centered
- Structure: two-column masthead (gauge left, statement right) → single bento row of Finance + Habits

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: warmth, editorial confidence, reads as "considered" not "dashbordy"
- Weak at: can feel slightly traditional; serif numerals less "data-tool" than mono

### Best for
Founders / content clients who value craft and writing-led brands; portfolios aiming for a calm, premium read.
