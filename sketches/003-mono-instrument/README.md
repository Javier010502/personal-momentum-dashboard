## Variant 003 — Mono Instrument

### Design stance
Everything in monospace, treated like a physical instrument dial. The gauge is a full radial dial with tick marks, evoking a gauge cluster rather than a chart. Amber accent.

### Key choices
- Accent: amber `#C2691C` (warm, distinctive, not blue/orange-gradient)
- Type: IBM Plex Mono for display/labels; IBM Plex Sans for body; all-caps technical micro-labels
- Gauge: full 360-degree radial dial with 40 tick marks, two thin concentric guide rings, centered mono numeral
- Structure: two-column masthead (dial left, tight mono statement right) → bento row

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: most distinctive / "signature" feel; reads as a crafted instrument, very portfolio-memorable
- Weak at: monospace body at length can feel less human; risk of feeling too "dev-tool" for content-brand clients

### Best for
Standing out in a portfolio; clients who appreciate technical craft (engineers, indie hackers, data-led brands).
