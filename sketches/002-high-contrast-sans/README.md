## Variant 002 — High-Contrast Sans

### Design stance
All-grotesk, no serif anywhere. Maximum typographic contrast through weight and scale, with a forest/teal accent. The gauge is an open half-ring that sits low and wide.

### Key choices
- Accent: forest `#1F6F5C` (cool, calm, non-default)
- Type: Space Grotesk throughout (600 weight for display numbers, 500 body); JetBrains Mono for meta
- Gauge: open 180-degree half-ring arc at the bottom of a wide panel, heavy 11px stroke, huge 88px numeral
- Structure: two-column masthead (wide half-ring gauge left, tight statement right) → bento row

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: modern, neutral, "product" feel; reads as a real SaaS tool
- Weak at: less personality than the serif or mono takes; cool accent can feel a touch generic-tech

### Best for
SaaS / startup founder clients who want a clean product aesthetic they recognize as "their world."
